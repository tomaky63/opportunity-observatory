Printed page 4, PDF page 6 (zero-based page 5); extension and Early Postwar Programs

主張　CRS2000回顧はCalifornia、New York City、Philadelphiaの一部で長期の公費継続があり、他地域の一部では2〜10年の公費継続もあったと記述する。補助終了から全地域の必然退出を推論しない。

記述の種類：source_assertion · 支持関係：supports

California was the only state to continue the World War II era program indefinitely. New York City (which had received state rather than federal subsidy during the war) also continued a permanent and sizeable program and the Philadelphia, PA, school board maintained a small number of the Lanham-funded centers indefinitely. In the states of Washington, New York, Massachusetts (very limited) and in Detroit, MI, some public funding was continued for 2 to 10 years.
